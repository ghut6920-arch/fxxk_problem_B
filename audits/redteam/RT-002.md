## Findings and Counterexamples

### RT2-F1 — One successful rehearsal can falsely satisfy formal readiness

- Severity: `CRITICAL` for the formal-readiness gate only.
- Evidence: sections 6 and 10 admit that one Q3/Q4 rehearsal and sample p95/max cannot establish tail guarantees, yet the formal gate requires only an actually completed non-formal whole rehearsal. The reviewed model may issue up to 5,222 sequential Q4 requests, so correlated stalls, retries, and exit delay can dominate.
- Affected claim or component: formal-readiness evidence package, not P0–P2 or the C0 geometry proof.
- Counterexample: a quiet-window rehearsal succeeds; a later burst of correlated latency causes an unknown accepted action or missed exit during a scarce formal attempt.
- Required remediation: one success must not be sufficient. Define a repeated, stressed, per-problem readiness gate; retain all misses; require offline replay of correlated stalls/retry bursts; and block formal readiness on any unresolved deadline miss or unknown action. Any 70%-deadline or 2×-delay margin must be labeled an autonomous empirical safety choice, not a theorem.

### RT2-F2 — C1-SIM can pass only inside an invented favorable clock

- Severity: `MAJOR`.
- Evidence: section 5.2 fixes one synthetic request/decision rate while real C1 remains disabled as `UNKNOWN`; the six-case screen can still encourage further investment.
- Affected claim or component: C1 investment/advance gate, not the mechanism-correctness tests.
- Required remediation: separate mechanism benefit from operational qualification. Replace the single timing point as an investment gate with a symbolic or preregistered break-even region over request, retry, and decision costs. Advance real C1 only when an evidence-supported timing region overlaps the profitable region with declared margin.

### RT2-F3 — Sparse author-designed holdouts and unenforced oracle independence invite overfitting

- Severity: `MAJOR`.
- Evidence: hand-built fixtures/traces and six holdouts do not cross all interacting boundaries; “independent evaluator” is stated but not structurally required; unlimited repair cycles can repeatedly consume holdouts.
- Affected claim or component: correctness/performance promotion confidence.
- Required remediation: require a clause-derived oracle with separate ownership/dependencies, differential/exhaustive small-world checks, safety-relevant mutation/metamorphic tests, crossed geometry/protocol/count/deadline cases, a preregistered maximum material-repair cycle count, and at least one untouched independent challenge manifest before candidate promotion.

## Steelman

1. The design separates analytic correctness, synthetic comparison, rehearsal evidence, and formal authorization cleanly.
2. C0 provides a deterministic safety comparator with explicit coverage and virtual-budget certificates.
3. Truth-retention, false-completion, and ledger errors trigger suspension before performance interpretation begins.
4. Frozen paired cases and immutable holdouts constrain opportunistic tuning and survivor-only reporting.
5. Real and virtual clocks remain separate, preventing mathematical guarantees from masquerading as deadlines.
6. C1 retains C0 fallback guarantees and disables adaptation whenever real-time evidence remains unavailable.
7. C2 receives resources only after bounded evidence shows multi-step planning could add value.
8. Comprehensive logging, version pinning, and failure preservation create unusually strong reproducibility foundations.

## Strongest Counter-Argument

1. The formal gate accepts one successful rehearsal despite thousands of deadline-sensitive serial requests.
2. A lucky service window can certify readiness while decisive tail latency remains untested.
3. Synthetic C1 timing uses invented favorable constants, so enabled behavior may never exist operationally.
4. Six hand-designed holdouts cannot represent unknown worlds, errors, and interacting protocol boundaries.
5. Evaluator independence is requested but not structurally enforced, allowing shared specification bugs through.
6. Negative results default toward repairable scopes, making family hypotheses difficult to falsify decisively.
7. Repeated repairs consume time, expose holdouts, and reward preserving the incumbent architecture.
8. Without stronger gates, impeccable records can still support an operationally fragile formal choice.
