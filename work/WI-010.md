## Required Design Content

1. List each claim/unknown to validate and map it to the exact mathematical-plan section, risk, evidence type, and decision that the evidence can support.
2. Define staged gates, at minimum: static proof/arithmetic checks; offline geometry/state property tests; protocol/mock integration; non-formal simulator rehearsal; and a separately authorized formal-test readiness gate. Do not merge these evidence levels.
3. For C0, design checks for Q1/Q2 geometry, Q3 nine-point coverage, Q4 81-point directional coverage, boundary/grid-line/grid-vertex cases, 225-centre clear coverage, output-error tolerance, action accounting, retry semantics, and the 63000/81000-second analytic virtual bounds.
4. For C1, design truth-retention tests for every response branch, state-capacity/depth behavior, fixed-location error consistency, global count/type constraints, queue creation/cancellation, $L=2$ fairness, fallback-cost inheritance, virtual-budget rejection, and real-time `UNKNOWN` fallback behavior.
5. For C2, define only a gated evaluation plan: finite feedback partition coverage, terminal-bound validity, resource growth, and a prerequisite showing material value beyond C1 before any implementation/experiment allocation.
6. Specify adversarial and boundary scenario families without fabricating observed cases: distances at/around 5, 20, 1000, and 1500 metres; bearings at ±1° and the 0/360 seam; source positions on the disk/grid/cell boundaries; directional headings on visibility boundaries; 10 and 16 sources; mixed type-count extremes; channel order/switching; repeated IDs, rejected/uncertain requests, and deadline edges.
7. Define common comparators, controlled variables, seeds/identifiers, configuration capture, data/version hashes, commands/logs to retain later, and paired comparison rules. C1/C2 must not receive more favorable cases, budgets, or stopping conditions than C0.
8. Define correctness-first metrics and secondary efficiency metrics. Include missed-source/false-completion/unsafe-pruning counts, clear success/completeness certificate, virtual-time ledger error, requests/switches/path length, wall-clock request and solver distributions, timeouts/retries, and paired C1-vs-C0 benefit/cost. Do not invent thresholds without labeling their rationale and reopen condition.
9. Give explicit resource budgets and stopping rules for each stage, including immediate stop on any false completion, true-state exclusion, ledger mismatch, protocol ambiguity, or unbounded resource growth. Distinguish an implementation defect, parameter/configuration defect, model-variant defect, and model-family evidence.
10. State advance/revise/stop conditions. Passing design checks may authorize drafting a later `EXP-NNN/SPEC.md`; it does not itself approve implementation, rehearsal, formal tests, a model selection, or publication.
11. Reserve formal tests: no formal opportunity may be used for debugging, parameter tuning, or first integration. Define the evidence package required before the Technical Lead may propose a formal-test WI, while recognizing that actual authorization remains separate and user-controlled.
